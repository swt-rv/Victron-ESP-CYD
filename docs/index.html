Camper Power Dashboard

Victron MPPT + SmartShunt + Ruuvi tags on an ESP32 2.8" display

This page needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (or Chrome on Android with a USB OTG cable). Safari and Firefox can’t talk to USB devices.

1. Install the firmware

Plug the ESP32 display into your computer with a USB data cable, click a button, pick the serial port, and wait for it to finish. Your saved settings are kept when you update.

Your browser doesn’t support installing from this page. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

Use the second button only if the first install shows inverted colours (white background, blue looks orange, etc.).

2. Enter your device settings

Click Connect, choose the same USB port, and the page will list the Victron and Ruuvi devices the display can hear. Click one to fill in its address. Keys stay on your device and in this browser tab; nothing is uploaded.
